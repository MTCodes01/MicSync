How to use MicSync
Step-by-step guide with Neo the Lizard 🦎
Step 1 · Receiver Device

Start Speaker Mode on Desktop

Designate your laptop, desktop, or tablet as the audio speaker to receive real-time sound.

  • 💻 Click "Use as Speaker" on this computer screen.
  • ⏳ The status will say "Waiting for peer…" until your phone connects.
  • 🎧 Pro-Tip: Use headphones or external speakers to prevent acoustic feedback whistling!
Step 2 · Quick Pairing

Scan QR Code with Mobile Phone

Connect both devices to the same room seamlessly without typing any long addresses.

  • 📶 Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network.
  • 📷 Open your phone's camera and point it at the QR code on the desktop screen.
  • 📋 Alternatively, tap "Copy Link" to share the room URL via chat or email.
Step 3 · Transmitter Mode

Allow Microphone & Start Streaming

Use your smartphone as a high-fidelity wireless microphone.

  • 🎙️ On your phone, tap "Use as Microphone".
  • 🛡️ When your browser prompts you, tap "Allow" for microphone permissions.
  • 🚀 Tap "Start Streaming". Audio will stream directly to your desktop speaker!
Bonus · Troubleshooting & Tips

Visualizer & Helpful Hints

Everything you need to ensure crystal clear sound and troubleshoot issues.

  • 📊 Audio Visualizer: Check the green/purple audio waveform bar to confirm sound is active.
  • 🔒 Microphone Blocked? Tap the lock icon in your browser address bar to allow mic access.
  • ⚡ Low Latency: Direct peer-to-peer WebRTC connection ensures near-zero delay!
